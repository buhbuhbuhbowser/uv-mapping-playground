03 · "More than RGB" = more images on the same UVs

The geometry and the UVs do not change. We just sample extra images at those same (u,v) coords, each feeding a different physical property:

• Albedo — base color (the only thing plain RGB gives you).
• Normal — fakes bumps by tilting the surface normal per-pixel. No geometry added.
• Roughness — matte vs glossy, per pixel.
• Metalness — dielectric vs metal.
• AO — baked contact shadow in crevices.

Toggle each map and orbit the light. The bumps you'll see are entirely the normal map — the mesh is a smooth plane.
