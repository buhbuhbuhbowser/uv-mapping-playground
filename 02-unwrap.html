02 · Unwrapping = flattening 3D onto the UV square

Every built-in primitive already ships with a uv attribute — someone decided how to flatten it. The canvas below draws that mesh's actual triangles in UV space. That picture is the unwrap.

Watch for two things:

• Distortion — with the checkerboard, even squares = faithful mapping; squished/stretched squares (e.g. a sphere's poles) = the flattening had to cheat.

• Seams — edges where the surface was cut to lay flat (the back of the sphere, the cube's net). Textures can visibly break across a seam.
