01 · UVs are just 4 numbers per corner

This flat quad has 4 vertices. Each one carries a 2D coordinate (u, v) that says "sample the texture image here." The texture is a labeled grid so you can see exactly which corner lands where.

Drag the sliders to move one corner's UV. Watch the image slide and stretch — the geometry never moves, only the addresses into the texture change.

The square below is "UV space" (the texture). Red dots show where your 4 corners currently point.
